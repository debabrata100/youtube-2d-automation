## Known registration limitations

**Equal canvas dimensions do not imply exact geometric alignment.**

1. **Plume:** source visible bounds at alpha >128 are x305–1044/y55–967. Its basal tip is around (567,967), while the crater connection should be near (640,320). At native placement it covers the front of the mountain rather than emerging from its summit. The preview-only placement study resolves the coarse anchor/scale for inspection, but no fitted plume export is delivered. To keep a taller dramatic plume above the crater in the final 9:16 scene, explicitly plan additional headroom/framing rather than cropping or silently changing registration.
2. **Lava:** source visible bounds are x30–1109/y338–865, while the mountain ends at visible y792. At alpha >128, 78,736 lava pixels lie on the mountain silhouette and **24,165 lie outside it**; 22,384 are below the mountain base. Only **76.52%** of those lava pixels have silhouette support. The lower tips float beyond the mountain. Precise summit contact, front-rim occlusion, and terrain-following branch placement require a deterministic fitting pass. Clipping alone would truncate the streams instead of fixing their paths.
3. Silhouette support is a two-dimensional diagnostic, not proof that streams follow every gully or gravity-consistent surface elevation. The raw preview shows branches that still need local deformation or path/reveal authoring. Do not call the source file terrain-fitted.
4. **Animation layers:** the main plume has painted internal billows, not a fully separated cloud rig. The optional ash wisp supplies one independent smaller structure; more detailed sublayering can be authored later. Lava topology is baked into its PNG and still needs progressive reveal masks.

These limitations are reported under the user's instruction to preserve clean artwork when generation cannot guarantee exact alignment. No fitting or animation integration is started automatically. Review the material quality separately from the unresolved placement work.
