# Gahanarka content plan: topic backlog

Started 19 Nov 2026. These are ideas to revisit, not commitments. Before any topic becomes a script, re-run the usual checks: YouTube search suggestions (does anyone search for it?), competitor check (is it a well-known video we'd be remaking? rule 8), our angle (what do we add?), the demand check (recent uploads and outliers, see below), and `docs/YOUTUBE_COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md`.

(Mirror of the project doc `claude/content-plan.md`; keep both the same.)

## Positioning (agreed 21 Nov 2026)

**The viewer:** a curious 16–35-year-old who watches Kurzgesagt, Astrum or Vsauce and wants to *feel* a space idea, with real numbers behind it. A grown-up audience: never children's content.

**The promise:** "What would really happen if…? Space thought experiments, drawn by hand and checked against real science."

**Rules that follow from it**
1. **Every long episode is a space thought experiment.** The title doesn't have to start with "What if": a "Could…", "When…" or "Why…" question works when the episode turns it into an experiment (for example, rewinding the Moon).
2. **Shorts are mostly cut from a long episode**, with their own hook, pointing to it (Related video). Space news only for something big, and always pointing to an episode on the same subject.
3. **A recognisable look per series, variety inside each video** (backgrounds, structures, title shapes).
4. **Grown-up audience:** adult topics and stakes, dry humour, normal vocabulary, no children's music or toy sound effects, honest titles and thumbnails (audience checks in `docs/SCRIPT_CRAFT_CHECKLIST.md` and `docs/VISUAL_CHECKLIST.md`). Setting: "No, it's not made for kids", no tags aimed at children.
5. **How we'll know it works:** after 8–10 long videos, check returning viewers, what viewers watch next, and which series keep viewers longest (the analytics dashboard).

**The idea behind the three tracks:** we own one sky. Moon, Sun and Earth episodes link to each other, reuse our drawings (Moon, tilted Earth, eclipses, clock, town, Arka), and build a back catalogue that's hard to copy one video at a time.

**Rules for picking the order**
1. Alternate the title shape. Not three "What if…" titles in a row; "If the Sun Disappeared…" counts as one (repetition rule 13). Mix *What if…*, *Why…*, *Could…*, *When…*, *How we'd die…*.
2. Alternate tracks: Moon → Sun → Earth → Moon…
3. Prefer topics with a myth to bust or a surprising answer. That's our edge (Ep 4's tilt myth).
4. Prefer topics that reuse existing drawings. They're cheaper and faster.
5. **Demand over uniqueness (20 Nov 2026):** space "what if" angles get copied by many small channels within days, so a never-done topic is rare. Pick topics with proven demand (recent uploads from mid-size channels doing well) and win on execution: Arka, the hand-drawn style, story structure (`docs/SCRIPT_CRAFT_CHECKLIST.md`) and real sources.
6. **Win the angle in a crowded topic (21 Nov 2026):** if the plain explainer is already everywhere (for example "why the Moon is moving away"), don't make the explainer; turn it into a thought experiment nobody else frames that way.

Legend: **Debabrata** = Debabrata's idea, **Claude** = Claude's addition. Facts marked *(check)* must be verified when scripting.

## Episode ending: Arka's next question (from Ep 5 on, agreed 19 Nov 2026)

Every long episode ends with an open loop: Arka wonders about something that grows out of this episode, and the narrator promises it's next.

1. Payoff: the narrator sums up.
2. One emotional closing line (for example, "Look up tonight.").
3. **Arka's new question,** with a thought bubble sketching the next topic.
4. Narrator: "Good question, Arka. That's our next episode."
5. Arka: "Subscribe so you don't miss it!"
6. End card (12 s, end-screen elements).

Rules:
- **Only tease what's really next:** its script is at least drafted. Say "next", never a date.
- **The question must grow out of this episode** (M1's shorter past days → E1; E1's slow change → E7's one-night change).
- **Vary the wording and the picture every time.** Same shape, never the same lines or animation (repetition rule 13).
- **Keep steps 3–5 under about 10 seconds.**
- **When the teased episode goes live, switch the previous episode's end-screen video to it** (see the publishing checklist).

No more night sky by default: pick the page colour from the concept (paper notebook, warm dusk, ocean blue-green, Mars rust; night sky only when the dark of space is the point) and never more than two uploads in a row on the same background. No red text on dark pages. Full rule in `docs/EPISODE_VISUAL_LESSONS.md`; every shot checked with `docs/VISUAL_CHECKLIST.md`.
