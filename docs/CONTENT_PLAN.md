# Gahanarka content plan: topic backlog

Started 19 Nov 2026. These are ideas to revisit, not commitments. Before any topic becomes a script, re-run the usual checks: YouTube search suggestions (does anyone search for it?), competitor check (is it a well-known video we'd be remaking? rule 8), our angle (what do we add?), and `docs/YOUTUBE_COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md`.

**The idea behind the three tracks:** we own one sky. Moon, Sun and Earth episodes link to each other, reuse our drawings (Moon, tilted Earth, eclipses, clock, town, Arka), and build a back catalogue that's hard to copy one video at a time.

**Rules for picking the order**
1. Alternate the title shape. Not three "What if X disappeared" in a row (repetition rule 13). Mix *What if…*, *Why…*, *Can…*, *How we'd die…*.
2. Alternate tracks: Moon → Sun → Earth → Moon…
3. Prefer topics with a myth to bust or a surprising answer. That's our edge (Ep 4's tilt myth).
4. Prefer topics that reuse existing drawings. They're cheaper and faster.

Legend: **Debabrata** = Debabrata's idea, **Claude** = Claude's addition. Facts marked *(check)* must be verified when scripting.

## Track 1: the Moon (follows Ep 4, "What If the Moon Disappeared Tonight?")

| # | Working title | From | Angle / surprise | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Why is the Moon leaving us, and could it ever come back?** | Debabrata (merges "moon approaching instead of leaving" + "why away, not the opposite") | The Moon drifts away because Earth spins faster than the Moon orbits: the tidal bulge runs ahead and tugs it forward. It *could* come back if Earth's day got longer than a month. That happens to Mars's moon Phobos, which spirals inward and will break up. Far future: Earth and Moon lock at a day of about 47 of today's days *(check)*. | **Recommended next Moon episode.** It picks up Ep 4's twist ending directly, and nearly all its drawings already exist. Two of Debabrata's ideas become one stronger video. |
| M2 | **What if we borrowed the Moon for just one month?** | Debabrata | Not "gone forever" but gone and back: what breaks in 30 days (tides, coral spawning calendar, night hunting) and what snaps back. The return is the twist: does the Moon's return cause giant tides? | Too close to Ep 4 to make next. Make it later, and say clearly that it's different: a month, then the return. |
| M3 | **Could an asteroid destroy the Moon? Would Earth get rings or a second moon?** | Debabrata | No known asteroid is anywhere near big enough. The Moon takes hits all the time; that's what its craters are. Ejecta from big impacts can become small temporary companions: 2024 PT5 was likely lunar ejecta. | Good myth-buster. Connects to M4. |
| M4 | **Does Earth have more than one moon? (It sort of does)** | Debabrata ("Will Earth ever get more moons") | Earth picks up temporary "mini-moons": 2024 PT5 was captured on 29 Sep 2024 and left that November. Kamo'oalewa is a quasi-satellite, probably a chip off our Moon. | Strong title. Could also be a punchy Short. |
| M5 | What if Earth had two moons? | Claude | Two eclipses, double tides, a second clock. A classic question, but it lets us reuse the Ep 4 drawings to show what would change. | Crowded topic; needs a fresh angle. |
| M6 | Why do we only ever see one side of the Moon? | Claude | Tidal locking, the same braking that is slowing Earth. Ties M1 and E1 together. | Great as a Short (under 45 s). |
| M7 | What if the Moon were as close as the Space Station? | Claude | Popular question. The answer is that it breaks apart into rings (the Roche limit) long before it gets that close. | Check competitors first. |

## Track 2: the Sun (start in parallel so we own it early)

| # | Working title | From | Angle / surprise | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **What if the Sun vanished for one second? (The 8-minute ghost Sun)** | Debabrata ("take the Sun away for a moment") | Nothing happens for 8 minutes 20 seconds: light *and* gravity both travel at light speed, so Earth keeps orbiting a Sun that's already gone. Then it goes dark and Earth flies off in a straight line. Even one second away leaves a visible "gap" in sunlight that travels outward. | **Recommended first Sun episode.** Huge demand, and the "ghost Sun" is our hook. Many videos cover "the Sun disappears", so the lead has to be the 8-minute delay. |
| S2 | **If the Sun became a black hole, would we be sucked in?** | Debabrata (incl. time dilation) | Myth-bust: no. A black hole with the Sun's mass is about 6 km across *(check)*, and Earth's orbit would not change at all. Time dilation at Earth's distance would be practically zero. The real problem: no light, so Earth freezes. Also: the Sun is too small ever to become a black hole. | Great myth video; "would we be sucked in?" is what people actually believe. |
| S3 | **Can another star ever replace the Sun?** | Debabrata | No star will replace it, but one is coming close. Gliese 710 will pass about 10,000 AU from the Sun in about 1.3 million years, through the Oort Cloud, sending comets our way. Scholz's Star passed about 0.8 light-years away 70,000 years ago. | Real, recent (Gaia) science, and few videos cover it. |
| S4 | The Sun is white, not yellow | Claude | Myth-bust: from space the Sun is white; our air makes it look yellow. | Perfect Short, cheap to make. |
| S5 | What if a solar storm like 1859 hit today? | Claude | The Carrington Event: telegraphs sparked and auroras were seen near the equator. Today it would hit power grids, satellites and GPS. | Timely while the Sun is still near its activity peak *(check)*. |
| S6 | Will the Sun swallow Earth? | Claude | The red giant phase in about 5 billion years. Scientists are genuinely unsure whether Earth gets swallowed. Ties to Ep 4's ending ("the Sun grows old"). | Clear Sun-track ending episode. |

## Track 3: Earth

| # | Working title | From | Angle / surprise | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **When will a day be 25 hours long?** | Debabrata | Days are lengthening by a couple of milliseconds per century (estimates range from about 1.7 to 2.3), so a 25-hour day is roughly 200 million years away. In the dinosaurs' time a day was shorter. Twist: melting ice is now changing day length too. | **Recommended first Earth episode.** Reuses the Ep 4 clock and Moon drawings, has a clear answer, and links to M1. |
| E2 | **How would we die if the Moon fell on Earth?** | Debabrata | ⚠ Kurzgesagt's "What Happens if the Moon Crashes into Earth?" (21.8M views) owns this. A remake risks rule 8. Only possible angle: the Moon never reaches the ground; it shatters into rings first (Roche limit), so the danger is the falling ring debris. | **Hold.** Do it only with a clearly different story, and well after M1. |
| E3 | **If an asteroid hit again, would dinosaurs come back?** | Debabrata | Twist: dinosaurs never fully died out, because birds *are* dinosaurs. Evolution doesn't repeat itself, so T. rex wouldn't come back; what fills the gap depends on who survives (probably small animals, like last time). Our T. rex drawing already exists. | Strong "how we'd die" title plus a myth-bust ending. Keep it non-graphic. |
| E4 | What if Earth stopped spinning for one second? | Claude | Everything not bolted down keeps moving at up to about 1,670 km/h at the equator *(check)*. A very popular question, so we need our own angle. | Check competitors first. |
| E5 | What if Earth's magnetic field flipped tonight? | Claude | Compasses point the wrong way and auroras appear everywhere. It has happened many times; the last was about 780,000 years ago *(check)*. | Also good for a Short. |
| E6 | What if Earth had no tilt? | Claude | No seasons. A natural sequel to Ep 4's tilt chapter, reusing the tilted-Earth drawing. | Cheap to make. |

## Suggested order (to decide later)

1. **M1** Why is the Moon leaving us, and could it come back? (a *Why* title, straight after Ep 4)
2. **S1** The 8-minute ghost Sun (a *What if* title; starts the Sun track)
3. **E1** When will a day be 25 hours long? (a *When* title; starts the Earth track)
4. **S2** Would a black-hole Sun suck us in? (myth-bust)
5. **M4** Does Earth have more than one moon?
6. **E3** Would dinosaurs come back?

Each long episode gets 2–3 Shorts, each with its own hook, layout and ending. Good Short-only ideas: **S4** (Sun is white), **M6** (one side of the Moon), **E5** (magnetic flip), M4 as a teaser.
