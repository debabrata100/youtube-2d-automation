# E7 Apophis: viewer's review and visual rework plan (23 Nov 2026)

Debabrata watched the full render (`jobs/e7-full`, 9:57): "very educational, but no visual attraction for viewers; it will only
retain viewers who need knowledge". His direction: **whatever we promise we deliver; we talk about destruction, so we show it;
the narrator can pause while the action plays with sound; everything the narrator says is shown with animation; interest is kept
to the end. That is how 2D animation proves itself against 3D.** Rules saved in `docs/VISUAL_CHECKLIST.md` §10.

The narration stays exactly as recorded (paid, approved). Pauses for action beats are cut into it for free
(`scripts/cut-narration.py`, `gapAfter`), like the brand-sting gap. Total runtime grows by about 35–45 s (to ~10:40).

## What has to be built (reusable for every episode)
- **Action-beat pauses:** a plan-script helper that adds `gapAfter` pauses to the narration cut and gives the storyboard the matching frames (free).
- **Camera:** shake, whip-pan and chase presets on top of push/drift; a per-shot default that is never static.
- **New kit pieces:** expanding shockwave ring, debris/dust burst, tsunami wave (rise and break), telescope and observatory dome, headline card (viral-video style), paper map, lottery machine, photo plate, calendar page flying off, rewind lines, size line-up helper.
- **Sound:** new synthesized cues (crack, splash, tear, wind, ding) next to rumble/boom/whoosh/riser.
- **Attraction report** in the sweep: picture changes a minute, longest still stretch, on-screen words a minute, static-camera shots, action beats per chapter.
- **Real images:** NASA/JPL Goldstone radar dish photo and OSIRIS-REx at Bennu (public domain); Debabrata downloads them into `assets/nasa/` (the workspace can't reach NASA's site).

- **Schedule:** if the sample is approved by 25 Nov, E7 can still go public on Thu 3 Dec; otherwise Sun 6 Dec.
