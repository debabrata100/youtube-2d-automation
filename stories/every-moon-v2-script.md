# "How You'd Die on Every Moon in the Solar System": script draft 5 (rework)

Long episode, 16:9, target **9–10 minutes**. Narrator: **Gahanarka R1**. Arka: **Arka D2**. Replaces draft 4 (`stories/every-moon-script.md`, narrated with Bill and Jessica, kept as the record).

**Why the rework (Debabrata's review, 21 Nov 2026):** the old video gave away its own mysteries (moon names and planets on screen during the hook; the "deadliest" and "you can fly" cards showed Io and Titan in the first minute), nothing built towards the climax, every moon looked the same, and Arka's lines were childish for our grown-up audience.

**Devices that build tension (new):**
1. **Two locked cards** from the cold open: **DEADLIEST** and **YOU CAN FLY**. They stay locked (a "?" silhouette) until their reveal (e48, e56).
2. **Arka's apartment hunt:** a checklist of moons she's considering; each one gets crossed off with a reason. The two locked slots sit at the bottom of her list.
3. **A spoken "survival time" for every moon**, shown on a tag that changes colour from green to red as the danger rises.
4. **The radiation countdown** (two months → one day → three hours) as the climax, with the alarm getting faster.

**Visual rule for this episode:** no moon names or planets on screen until the narration names them; a colour per moon (VISUAL_CHECKLIST §2); no red text.

## Honesty notes

- "A dozen moons" = our Moon, Callisto, Phobos, Deimos, Miranda, Enceladus, Triton, Charon, Ganymede, Europa, Io, Titan (12).
- Survival times are for an unshielded person standing on the surface in our 8-hour suit; the radiation times use draft 4's dose figures (≈ 5 Sv as deadly). Said as "about".
- "Deadliest" = fastest to kill a suited person, which is how the episode defines it (e5–e6).
