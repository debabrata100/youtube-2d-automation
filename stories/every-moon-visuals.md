# Every Moon: one-visual-per-sentence sheet (M3.7, step 1)

Draft 1 · 2026-11-16 · follows Script Draft 4

**Rules** (from the Paint Explainer study):
- a new picture about every 5 s;
- every sentence shown literally;
- the moon name stays at the top of every frame;
- each moon has its own ground colour, with its parent planet in the sky;
- numbers appear on screen;
- one visual gag per moon;
- a NASA photo now and then as proof.

**Asset codes:**
- **E** = existing kit (figure poses float, tumble, flap, walk, run, lie, cower, panic; wings, mask, coat, helmet; counter, thermometer, stamp, pressure-arrows, motion-lines, exclaim, question, cross, check, vignette; particles, puffs, glow, terrain, sky, planet, mountain, water, cloud).
- **N** = new prop to build (list at the end).
- **P** = public-domain NASA photo insert, credited on screen "NASA/JPL".

**Arka:** gold suit. **"You":** a generic white-suit astronaut, like the viewer-astronaut in the 13M video. Arka is the commentator; "you" is the one who dies.

7. `airbag` (inflate, bounce) and `airbag-pack`.
8. `small-moon` (lumpy potato shape).
9. `ice-cutaway` (shell, ocean, depth arrows).
