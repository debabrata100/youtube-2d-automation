**Pays off Ep 4's ending:** "Next time on Gahanarka: we take away something even bigger."

## Honesty notes

- **The premise is impossible** (mass can't vanish; general relativity doesn't allow it). We say so plainly in s65 rather than hiding it, as Ep 4 did with the Moon.
- **Temperature timelines differ between scientists** (Summers vs Stevenson). We use round numbers and attribute the first one to "one planetary scientist" (s40). We don't claim precise days.
- **"Seven hundred and forty meters"** is our own calculation (simple Newtonian physics, shown above) so viewers can check it.
- **The open-loop rule:** only tease what's really next. s67 teases **M1, "Why is the Moon leaving us, and could it come back?"**. M1's script must be **at least drafted before Ep 5 is published**, or s67–s68 change.
