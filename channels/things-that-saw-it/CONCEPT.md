# Things That Saw It (working name): channel concept

**Status: dropped on 26 Nov 2026.** Debabrata decided against it the same day: to stay safe from YouTube's mass-produced-content rule it would need a low cadence (one film every 1-2 weeks) plus new character-animation work, and the time is better spent on Gahanarka. Kept for reference; the multi-channel layout in `channels/` stays for any future channel.

Originally proposed on 26 Nov 2026 as a second channel next to Gahanarka.

## The idea
Short 2D animated films (2-5 minutes) about the small moments that change an ordinary person's life, each **told by an
object that watched it happen**: the piano nobody plays, the running shoes by the door, the phone with the unsent
message, a dad's old bike. The object narrates in first person ("Eleven years. Nobody lifted my lid."), so every film
has a clear point of view, a natural title and a thumbnail hero.

## Why (evidence, 26 Nov 2026)
Last Soul (@lastsoulorigin, 20.8K subscribers from 14 uploads in about two months) proves the demand for short
emotional life-lesson films: "1 MINUTE | An Animated Short Film" reached 1.3M views (their next best: 70K, 48K;
most films 8-23K). It works because almost everyone has quit a big plan, the fix is tiny and believable, and it ends
hopeful. The same "small number + human stakes" hook worked for our Miranda Short.

## How we stay original (compliance rule 8: never remake a competitor's format in their style)
- **Narrator is the object**, never the person. Their films are told from the person's side.
- **No faceless black-silhouette characters with dot eyes** (their look). People appear small, partial or out of
  focus; the object is the hero of every frame and thumbnail.
- **No "ONE WORD | An Animated Short Film" titles.** Our titles are the object's line ("11 Years Unplayed").
- Do not reuse their story beats (the running plan, the noodle shop, the temper, the phone at dinner).

## Look
Warm storybook paintings drawn in our own code: soft window light, dust in the light, wood and paper textures, grain,
a vignette. Bold Poppins lettering, at most 4 words, on thumbnails. Calm piano or acoustic music.

## Release rhythm (to start)
One film every 1-2 weeks plus one 30-50 s Short per film. A Short is its own story beat, not a trimmed trailer
(their trailer Shorts get 3-5K views).

## Timing
Habit and New-Year-resolution stories peak from late December to mid-January: aim the first film for about
26-28 Dec 2026.

## First film: "11 Years Unplayed" (about 3 min)
An upright piano in a hallway narrates. A girl quits lessons; the piano watches eleven years pass (boxes, dust, a
photo on its lid). As an adult she comes home, sits down and plays for one minute. The next night, two.
Ending line: "She never got good. She just came back every day."
Folder: `films/01-eleven-years-unplayed/`.
