Most videos say that without the Moon, Earth would tip over. A famous 1993 study found the tilt could swing anywhere from 0° to 85°. But in 2012, NASA scientist Jack Lissauer and his team ran far more detailed simulations: Earth would wobble more, but stay roughly between 12° and 38°, even over 100 million years. No sudden flip.

Sources:
• Laskar, Joutel & Robutel (1993), Nature 361
• Lissauer, Barnes & Chambers (2012), Icarus

Full episode: What If the Moon Disappeared Tonight?
