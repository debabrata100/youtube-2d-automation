Most videos say that without the Moon, Earth would tip over. A famous 1993 study found the tilt could swing anywhere from 0° to 85°. Sources:
• Laskar, Joutel & Robutel (1993), Nature 361
• Lissauer, Barnes & Chambers (2012), Icarus
