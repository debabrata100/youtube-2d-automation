## SEO: what matters, in order

1. **Click-through rate (title plus thumbnail) and retention decide reach.** Text metadata only tells YouTube what the video is about; viewer behaviour decides how far it goes. Our retention is already strong, so the title and thumbnail are our lever.
2. **Titles use words people actually type.** I took every keyword below from YouTube's own search suggestions on 17 Nov, for example "how you would die on every planet", "dying on every moon", "what happens if you take off your helmet in space" and "can humans live on titan". The main keyword goes near the front of the title. Titles stay under about 60 characters so they don't get cut off on phones.
3. **Descriptions run 150–300 words.** The keyword goes in the first sentence, written as plain English rather than keyword stuffing. Sources and a link to the related video go further down.
4. **Captions:** upload our SRT every time. Captions also give YouTube the full transcript.
5. **Chapters** on long videos: start at 0:00, each at least 10 s, at least 3 of them.
6. **Hashtags:** 2–3 relevant ones at the end of the description. The first three show above the title.
7. **Tags matter little now.** Paste the short list, but don't spend time on them.
8. **Every video points to another:**
   - Shorts get a *Related video* link (the only clickable link a Short has) and a pinned comment.
   - Long videos get an end screen.
   - Everything goes in the playlist **"Could You Survive…?"**, which already exists.
9. **Shorts and long videos are ranked separately.** A weak Short won't hurt the long videos, so it's safe to test Shorts freely.
