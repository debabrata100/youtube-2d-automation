## Review (17 Nov)

**Ready to upload.** The facts match the long video, the narration is the approved one, and the captions are timed to the voice. The long version holds 83% of its viewers, so the script itself works.

Weaker points, because this Short was built before the Episode 3 upgrades:
- **The first 2 seconds are a plain stick figure on a white page.** In the Shorts feed, the first second decides whether people swipe away, and this one gives them no reason to stay.
- **At 64 s it runs a little long.** Shorts get full reach up to 3 minutes, but they finish more often under about 50 s.
- **Small labels** ("Moon / Mars", "pressure suit") are hard to read on a phone.

I would **upload it as is** and treat it as our first test of the Shorts feed. The lessons (a cold open on the payoff image, bigger labels, under 50 s) go into the Miranda Short. Re-editing this one would take about an hour of free work. It's worth doing only if this Short performs badly.
